Tonight’s quiet tale

Cesare and the Lantern Forest

When the sky became blue and silver, Cesare found a little lantern waiting beside his bed. It was not too bright, not too loud, but warm enough to show the way.

The lantern whispered, “Tonight, we do not need to run. We only need to wonder.” So Cesare followed its soft glow into a forest where every tree remembered a dream.

There he met a tiny fox who was afraid of the dark. Cesare smiled, held the lantern gently, and together they discovered that the dark was not empty at all. It was full of stars waiting to be seen.

And when the moon rose high above the trees, Cesare felt calm, brave, and ready to sleep. The lantern blinked once, like a goodnight kiss, and the forest became a dream.
